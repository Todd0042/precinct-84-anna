# Precinct 84 — Cross-Tier Feature Parity Rule

Any feature, setting, QoL control, or generation option added to Precinct 84 MUST be implemented cleanly across **all 3 tiers** within the boundaries of what each tier is defined to be:

- **Tier 1 (Co-Pilot):** Authored Case I + on-demand `"Ask Claire"` tactical AI hints + Case II AI sequel unlock on victory (`no` upfront AI story generation).
- **Tier 2 (Intermissions / Mixed):** Authored Case I + AI "between-calls" dramatizations bridging Case I acts + Case II AI sequel unlock on victory.
- **Tier 3 (Full AI Director):** 100% AI-generated campaign starting from Turn 1.

Every playback control (TTS, Auto-Play Sequential/AI, Pause/Resume), generation granularity option (`1-by-1 / Ch x Ch` vs `Full`), Story Bible continuity, asset pool constraint, and save/delete/export workflow must apply cleanly to every tier where that capability exists.
